Privacy information

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Information stored on your device

Your language and display preferences, saved stories, reading history, and cached article details are stored locally on your device. You can manage or delete this information in the app's Settings. No account is needed to use the app.

Connections to other services

The app connects directly to third-party publishers to retrieve news and, when enabled, images. These services may receive technical information such as your IP address and may handle it under their own privacy policies. The app contains promotional content and links to external websites; those websites have their own practices.

Search and promotion

Search terms are used on your device to filter stories. The app may display a promotional banner based on characters in your search; it does not send the search term to a separate advertising service for this purpose.
